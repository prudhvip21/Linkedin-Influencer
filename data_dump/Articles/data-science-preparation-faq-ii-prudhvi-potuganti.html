      How does industry work? Does the data sets in industry look like Kaggle one’s or hackathons? How much % of projects are deployed? Is the boom real?

The industry is still in nascent stage when it comes to Data Science implementation. But there are industries like insurance, finance which have deployed predictive analytics long back. For example, the premium you pay or the stock prices prediction for next day or electrical load forecasting etc are the problems which are solved statistically decades ago.

The new age E Commerce, retail industries driven by scale of data they capture, realized statistical way of solving predictive problems is no longer working. Hence, they shifted to data intensive techniques like Machine Learning. The conclusion is classification and regression are not 21st century problems. I hope you realize that only the SCALE is new and Machine Learning usage is new not the Machine Learning in itself.

To answer about implementation part – industry is aggressively implementing it. But there is a long way to go. The usage of word ‘boom’ is justified to an extent because industry hasn’t yet extensively monetized AI. Investments are being poured in , with an optimistic view of ROI.

Countless companies which sit on huge piles of data are hiring data scientist teams aggressively and getting managers deliver POCs to top management. Less than 10% of such projects are getting deployed as per my observation. Less than 1% are monetized. To be honest, we are still struggling. It's not as rosy as pointed out there by click bait journalists or certificate providers. ( This is my personal perspective)

Coming to technical challenges in Industry projects, lack of relevant data is predominant issue in the small and mid-scale companies. MNCs or companies directly working on same domain/customers/clients have access to rich data. Even in such cases, the format may be different, or data is stored in multiple formats and multiple locations or it may be extremely untidy. Data is a major challenge in industry which Kaggle won't show you. Next problem is – cleaning data and putting in required format. In case of hackathons or Kaggle , the data is mostly tidy. So Kaggle doesn’t reflect industry as a whole.

Industry problems are not always well defined. The end goal is often vague. It is not the case when you start off with ackathons where problem statment is well defined. Results too are often not as good as you get in hackathons. The expectations are always high. You need to have very good story telling and convincing skills to sell the results across multiple teams in your company. The visualisations need to be profound.

When it comes to deploying models, if the results are just another table you save in data base, then it is easy to plug into back end and trigger through API whenever needed. If it is a web based solution, there are APIs or packages which can run your models real time. Of course, I am not talking about Google, Facebook, Amazon which develop their own solutions for deploying AI solutions. And deploying - Data Science solution is ocean which is very very diverse.

All opinions are personal and has to be taken as sample rather than population. :)
