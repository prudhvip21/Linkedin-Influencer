I have been getting a lot of questions about Data Science Preparation. Hence, I would like to write answers from my perspective. All views expressed are personal and can be considered as a sample opinion rather than population opinion

I learnt concepts from free online courses– and I am struggling to get interview calls. Even if I get , I am not able to convert them . This was the most frequent question among my replies and the rot cause for the problem is lack of relevant experience and practice on use cases

Let’s divide the problem into two parts – getting interview calls and converting them

1.     How to get interview calls :

This is subjective. Work on your profile hard. Having diverse skill-set on resume really helps. Most of the times, recruiters at fist sight are keen on diverse skills rather than depth of skills. Learn more tools even it is just at surface level. Also, don’t send a generic CV to every recruiter. Look for JD and then fine tune your resume according to JD if it fits and send it across. It requires extra effort to prepare resume for every JD but it is worth doing it.

The project/s you put on CV makes or breaks the decision of your interview call. Make sure you have one extensive/in-depth project and 2-3 projects intermediate projects added to your resume. Clearly mention your contribution or results achieved. Domain of your projects also plays a role. My personal recommendation for every job seeker is to choose one project from eCommerce , one from finance, another from health care domains etc. The reason is that recruiter gets excited to look at projects from their own domain.

Also invest time in carefully crafting a visual resume. Your resume should speak of your skills. Believe it or not, I designed a visual resume sitting for 16 hours. And I applied to a company which gets 2,000 applicants for a single position and I got shortlisted. In case, you need help, I can guide you in fine tuning your resume.

2.     After getting interview call

  Revise all concepts before you go to interview. I recommend keeping a cheat sheet for all concepts so that you can revise as soon as possible whenever there is interview. Don’t ever commit mistakes in basics. Even a small mistake can result in rejection. If not confident, please let interviewer know instead of confidently making a mistake. Also, if you need more preparation time, always seek time.

Be thorough with projects you have put on resume. Strictly NO to cooked up projects on resume. You will be grilled over each project and recruiters are very good at finding out cooked up projects. Cooked up projects can result to a straight rejection. Even you have one project under your kitty, be happy with it and speak confidently about what have you done and your learning.

Most of the time, your confidence, efforts, perseverance , attitude play key role in final selection. Focus on letting recruiter know you possess them.

  To reach out more people, I recommend building a good LinkedIn profile. Reach out to key people who share posts on recruitment. Keep connecting with data scientists and recruiters. Keep liking the work your co workers put on it. Interact with comments and views. Seek their code/data set and improve. You can show case you content too.
